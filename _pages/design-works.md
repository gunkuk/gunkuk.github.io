    <h3>Megastructure — Material System &amp; Superstructure</h3>
    <p class="project-meta">Yonsei University Studio X &middot; Year 3, Fall 2024</p>
    <p>
      Hypar-based unit system applied to the Yeouido Park site. The project explores
      a structural and spatial logic derived from modular unit aggregation, where the
      material system itself generates the superstructural form.

  <div class="project-entry">
    <h3>EHLA House &amp; Office — Concrete Tectonics with Seoul Skyline</h3>
    <p class="project-meta">Yonsei University Studio X &middot; Year 3, Spring 2024</p>
    <p>
      Mixed-use residential and office program situated on a retaining-wall terrain
      in Jongno-gu. The design engages the topographic condition of the Seoul hillside
      through concrete tectonic expression, creating a sectional dialogue between
      dwelling, work, and the city skyline.
    </p>
